# Wallet & Billing

## 2-2-1 Payments

### wal-001
Q: Where can I see what plan I'm on and when I'll be billed next?
A: Go to Settings, then Billing. Billing has three sections: Subscriptions, Payments, and Wallet and Transactions. You'll land on Subscriptions, which shows your plan, how much it costs per month, and your next billing date.

### wal-002
Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: You can modify or cancel your plan from the Subscriptions page under Settings, then Billing. Before you cancel, message REIblast support and let them know what's going on so they can help get you taken care of.

### wal-003
Q: Where can I see my charges?
A: Go to Settings, then Billing, then Payments. All your charges show up there, including wallet recharges, usage charges, and subscription charges. Your subscription invoices are listed under Invoices.

### wal-004
Q: How do I update my card on file?
A: Go to Settings, then Billing, then Payments. Click the pencil icon next to your payment method and enter your new card.

### wal-005
Q: Why is my contact not in my workflow anymore?
A: If there was an error while sending to that contact, such as your wallet running out of funds or hitting your daily limit, the SMS error catch-all adds an SMS fail tag and removes the contact from the workflow. The contact keeps the tag for the message it was on, so when you add it back to the blast, it picks up where it left off, as long as you don't change its tags. It's a backup for emergencies, not something to rely on, so keep an active, funded card on file so your sends don't fail in the first place.

### wal-006
Q: Why was my account paused?
A: Your account gets paused for nonpayment if your subscription payment fails even once, or if a wallet auto recharge fails three times. Make sure you have an active, unlocked card with money on it linked to your account.

### wal-007
Q: What do I need to keep on my card so my account keeps running?
A: Keep an active card on file that's unlocked, has money on it, and has the correct card information. That card pays for your entire system, and if a payment fails, your texts fail. A single failed subscription payment, or three failed wallet recharges, will pause your account.

### wal-008
Q: Can I use my Cash App card for REIblast?
A: Yes. Just make sure there's money in your Cash App, the card is unlocked, and the card information on file is correct.

## 2-2-2 Wallet and Transactions

### wal-009
Q: What is my wallet?
A: Your wallet is a prepaid balance in dollars. Every text, call, email, and anything else that costs money inside REIblast draws from it, including the one-time A2P registration fee. When your balance runs low, auto recharge tops it up from your card on file.

### wal-010
Q: Where do I find my wallet?
A: Go to Settings, then Billing, then Wallet and Transactions.

### wal-011
Q: Did I spend more this month than last month?
A: Check the usage overview, which is the first thing you see in Wallet and Transactions. It compares this month to last month, shows the products you use the most, and includes a six-month chart.

### wal-012
Q: What am I spending the most money on?
A: The usage overview shows your top products by spend. For a full breakdown, open By Activity, which lists everything you can do in the system, how much you spent on each, and what percent of your bill it made up.

### wal-013
Q: Where do I see my A2P registration fee?
A: It shows up in By Activity, under Settings, then Billing, then Wallet and Transactions, alongside everything else that's been pulled from your wallet.

### wal-014
Q: How much money is in my wallet?
A: Open Wallet & Recharge in Wallet and Transactions. It shows your full balance breakdown: your wallet total, any credits, and your total balance. Your auto recharge settings and top-up history are there too.

### wal-015
Q: How can I see every charge to my account?
A: Open Transactions in Wallet and Transactions. Every charge gets its own row with a reference ID, the date it was billed, the activity date, a description, the amount, and a running total of your wallet balance.

### wal-016
Q: What are these carrier fees next to my texts?
A: The phone carriers charge a fee for every message you send. It's charged by the carriers, not by REIblast, and it's a normal part of business texting. Most of the time carrier fees don't appear in your transactions summary at all.

## 2-2-3 Wallet Settings

### wal-017
Q: What is auto recharge?
A: Auto recharge automatically adds funds to your wallet so your balance doesn't hit zero in the middle of a blast. It's on by default and can't be turned off; you can only change how it works.

### wal-018
Q: Can I turn off auto recharge?
A: No. Auto recharge is on by default, and the only thing you can change is how much it charges and when it triggers.

### wal-019
Q: Where do I change my auto recharge settings?
A: Go to Settings, then Billing, then Wallet and Transactions, and open Wallet & Recharge. The auto recharge section there controls how much is charged and when.

### wal-020
Q: What does the recharge amount mean?
A: The recharge amount is how much is charged to your card to refill your wallet each time your balance hits your low balance limit. It defaults to $10, and you can set it anywhere from $10 to $15,000. Most members keep it at $10.

### wal-021
Q: Should I set my recharge amount higher or lower?
A: Either is fine; set it to what's comfortable for you. A smaller amount like $10 means more frequent, smaller charges, while a larger amount like $50 means fewer, larger charges.

### wal-022
Q: What is the low balance limit?
A: The low balance limit is the balance that triggers an auto recharge. When your wallet falls below it, your recharge amount is charged. It defaults to $0, and you can set it anywhere from $0 to $1,000.

### wal-023
Q: What should I set my low balance limit to?
A: It's up to you. At $0, the system waits until your wallet is completely used up before it recharges. At something higher like $10, your wallet usually never drops below $10 before another recharge is charged.

### wal-024
Q: What are the best auto recharge settings?
A: The recommendation is to leave the defaults alone: a $10 recharge amount and a $0 low balance limit.

### wal-025
Q: Why does my wallet balance show negative?
A: Your wallet is prepaid, but it can go slightly negative while you're using the system. When it drops below your low balance limit, auto recharge kicks in to bring it back up.

### wal-026
Q: Why was I charged a little more than my recharge amount?
A: If your wallet went negative, the recharge covers that negative amount first and then adds your recharge amount. For example, at a balance of negative $0.45 with a $10 recharge amount, you're charged $10.45, which brings you back to zero and then adds $10.

## 2-2-4 Adding Funds Manually

### wal-027
Q: How do I add money to my wallet?
A: Go to Settings, then Billing, then Wallet and Transactions. Click Add next to your balance, enter the amount you want to add, and click Add. Your card is charged right away and the funds are added to your wallet.

### wal-028
Q: When should I add funds to my wallet manually?
A: Add funds before a big blast so none of it fails from low funds. Also add funds if you see that a recharge was attempted and didn't go through, for example because your card was locked.

### wal-029
Q: I added funds but my balance didn't change. What's wrong?
A: The new balance can take a moment to show up. Refresh the page to see it.

### wal-030
Q: Do I have to be on the Wallet & Recharge screen to add funds?
A: No. Your balance and the Add button appear on every view inside Wallet and Transactions, so you can add funds from any of them.

### wal-031
Q: Why would I need more money in my wallet as I go?
A: As you move up through the messaging limit levels, you're able to send more and more texts, which uses more wallet funds. Keep an eye on your balance and add funds when you're planning a bigger blast.

## 2-2-5 Putting It All Together

### wal-032
Q: How does billing work in REIblast?
A: Your card on file is the source of all the money in your account. It pays your subscription directly, and it funds your wallet through auto recharge or when you add funds manually. Your wallet then pays for usage, and every text, call, and email draws from it bit by bit. If anything in your billing ever looks off, check your card on file first.

### wal-033
Q: Does my wallet pay for my subscription?
A: No. Your subscription and your wallet don't share funds. Your subscription is charged directly to your card on file, and your wallet is funded from your card separately. Your wallet only pays for usage like texts, calls, and emails, so adding funds to your wallet won't cover your subscription.

### wal-034
Q: What happens if there's no money on my card?
A: Without funds on your card, your subscription can't be paid and your wallet can't be refilled. With no money in your wallet, you can't blast.

### wal-035
Q: Is there a cheat sheet for how billing and the wallet work?
A: Yes. There's a downloadable Wallet & Billing quick reference guide (PDF) in the course resources that covers your card on file, subscription, wallet, auto recharge, and transactions at a glance.
