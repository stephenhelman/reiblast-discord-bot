# Account & Login

## 1-2 Logging In

### acc-001
Q: Where do I go to log in to REIblast?
A: Go to app.reiblast.app, which is where you sign in to the CRM. If you forget that address, go to reiblast.app (the site you used to sign up) and click Log In, which takes you to the same login page. Bookmark app.reiblast.app so you always have it.

### acc-002
Q: I just signed up and was never given a password. How do I log in for the first time?
A: On your first login you only have your email, no password. On the login page, click Forgot Password, enter the email you used to set up your account, and click Reset Password. Open the reset email, click Reset Password, and set your new password, then sign in with your email and that password.

### acc-003
Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
A: On the login page at app.reiblast.app, click Forgot Password, enter the email on your account, and click Reset Password. You'll receive a password reset link by email; click it and set a new password, then sign in.

### acc-004
Q: I requested a password reset but never got the email. What's wrong?
A: The most common cause is a typo or different email: if the email you enter doesn't match the one your account was set up with, a reset link will never be sent. If you've requested a reset and haven't received the link, contact support: text or call 915-800-8266 (text is preferred), email support@mail.reiblast.app, or message on Discord, and say you can't log in.

### acc-005
Q: It's asking me for a security code when I log in. Where does that come from?
A: After you enter your email and password, you're asked to have a security code sent to either your phone number or your email. Choose one, enter the code you receive, and you'll be taken into your account.

## 3-1 Adding Users

### acc-006
Q: Can my VA just log in with my account?
A: No. Anyone on your team, including a virtual assistant, should have their own user account instead of logging in under yours. You can add as many users as you need and give each one only the access they need.

### acc-007
Q: How do I add a user to my account?
A: Go to Settings, then Users, and add a new user. First name, last name, and email are required; a phone number is optional. Then choose their role and permissions, assign a phone number and call settings if needed, and save.

### acc-008
Q: How does a new user set up their login?
A: When you add a user, you can set a password for them yourself. If you don't, saving the user sends them an email so they can log in and set up their account.

### acc-009
Q: Should I make my team member an admin or a user?
A: Admin is selected by default and is meant for partners who are helping run the account and need a lot of access. For someone who's just coming in to work leads, choose User.

### acc-010
Q: How do I make it so my VA only sees the leads I assign them?
A: Edit the user, set their role to User, and restrict their data visibility to only assigned data. When they log in, they'll only be able to access the contacts you've assigned to them, and the same applies to opportunities and pipelines.

### acc-011
Q: How do I control what a user can do in my account?
A: In the user's roles and permissions, you can set what they're allowed to do, such as viewing and managing contacts, bulk actions (including uploading contacts), and access to opportunities and pipelines.

### acc-012
Q: How do I give a user their own phone number?
A: Edit the user and open Call & Voicemail. Assign them a phone number you've purchased, then choose how they receive calls to that number.

### acc-013
Q: Where do a user's calls ring?
A: Calls are forwarded based on what you choose when setting up the user. In the user's Call & Voicemail settings, after assigning them a number, pick how they receive calls, such as the LeadConnector app, or a desk phone if one is set up. By default, calls ring in the web app, and you can also set how long calls ring before going to voicemail.

### acc-014
Q: Can I connect a user's calendar?
A: Yes. In the user's settings you can set their availability and connect calendars to their account.

### acc-015
Q: How do I change or remove a user?
A: Go to Settings, then Users. Each user is listed with their name, email, phone number, and role, and you can edit or delete them from that screen at any time.

### acc-016
Q: Why would I add more than one user for texting?
A: You can split your text blasts across multiple users, each with their own phone number, to lighten the load on any single phone number.
